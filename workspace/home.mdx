After you activate a plan, Signyd opens **Home** (`/dashboard`).

The app shell includes **Home**, **Documents**, **Contacts**, **Templates**, **Settings** (hidden for members), **Account**, language, and **Sign out**.

## Home cards

Home summarizes your workspace so you can send the next request without hunting through lists.

Typical cards:

- Recent documents and their status
- Remaining **signing credits** and **ID verification credits**
- Shortcuts: **+ Document**, open templates, or go to billing if you are out of credits

Members see the same Home, documents, contacts, and templates. They do not see **Settings**.
