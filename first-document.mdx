---
title: "First document"
description: "Upload, add signers, place fields, and send in three steps."
---
You need an **active or trialing plan** and at least **one signing credit**. If any signer has **Require Signyd Verify**, you also need enough **ID verification credits**.
## Step 1 — Upload
1. Select **+ Document** (Home or Documents) or go to `/documents/new`.
2. Drag a file or click to browse.
3. Allowed formats: **PDF, DOC, DOCX**. Maximum size: **15 MB**.
4. Word files convert to PDF in the browser. Do not close the tab during **Uploading** or **Converting**.
5. Optional: **Open templates** and start from a saved layout.
<Warning>
Password-protected (open-encrypted) PDFs cannot be processed. Remove the open password and upload again.
</Warning>
## Step 2 — Recipients (`/documents/[id]/prepare`, step 1)
1. Add at least one signer with **email, first name, and last name**.
2. Use **+ Add recipient**, **Add myself**, or pick a saved contact.
3. Toggle **Require Signyd Verify** on any signer who must pass identity verification before the signing panel.
4. Continue.
If you started from a template, Signyd may require a minimum number of signers.
## Step 3 — Fields (step 2)
1. Select the recipient in the toolbar.
2. Choose a field type and click the page to place it.
3. Add at least one **Signature** or **Initials** field.
4. Open **Field settings** to mark required/optional, validation, size, and allowed signature methods.
See [Fields](/documents/fields) for every type.
## Step 4 — Review and send (step 3)
1. Confirm **From**, **Send to**, and optional **CC** (CC receives the final document only).
2. Optional: **Request signatures in order** (sequential signing).
3. Set **Document language** (English or Español). This controls emails, the signing legal notice, and the audit trail for this request.
4. Edit **Subject** and **Message**.
5. Optional: **After signing** redirect URL if your plan includes branding.
6. Select **Send document**. Wait for **Sending for signature…**. Do not leave the page.
### After send
- If you added yourself as a signer, Signyd can open your signing link immediately.
- Otherwise you land on the document detail page with recipient statuses and the signing links.
## If send fails
| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No signing credits remaining | Upgrade or buy a credit pack |
| No ID verification credits remaining | Buy a KYC pack or upgrade |
| Identity verification is not configured | Contact Signyd support |
| Sending is not configured yet | Contact Signyd support |

A **Buy signing or ID verification credits** overlay can open from the send flow.
