---
title: "Signer experience"
description: "What a recipient sees on the unique signing link."
---

Signers open a unique email link on `/sign/[token]`. They do **not** need a Signyd account.

## Steps

1. Open the email and select the signing link.
2. If **Require Signyd Verify** is on, complete identity verification first. See [Signyd Verify](/verify/identity).
3. Review the document and the electronic signature notice (in the **document language**).
4. Fill required fields and apply the signature or initials.
5. Confirm. Signyd records the event on the audit trail.

## Common blocks

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Identity required | The panel stays hidden until verification passes or the requester approves a review |
| Sequential signing | The signer waits until earlier recipients finish |
| Expired or voided | The link explains that the request is no longer open |

After everyone signs, recipients receive the completed document. Optional **After signing** redirect opens if the sender configured it.
