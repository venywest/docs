---
title: "Public Verify page"
description: "Anyone with the link can confirm a completed document."
---

Route: `/verify/[token]`

The public Verify page lets a reviewer check integrity and download the signed PDF for a limited time. Reviewers do not need a Signyd account.

## What reviewers see

- Document title and completion status
- Hash / integrity information (SHA-256 stored by Signyd)
- A time-limited download of the completed PDF (platform-sealed when sealing succeeded)

**ID photos and biometric data never appear on this page.** See the [Identity Verification and Biometric Policy](/verify).

## Expiration

Verify pages expire. Download links expire. After expiry the page explains that the link is no longer valid. Ask the sender for a new copy from the workspace if you still need the file.
