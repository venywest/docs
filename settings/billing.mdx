Tabs: **Billing**, **Plans & Addons**, **Cards**.

## Envelopes and credits

In the product UI, signing usage is shown as **envelopes**.

| Type | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Envelope** | **1 envelope = 1 completed signing process** (multi-party send) |
| **Monthly envelopes** | Included with the active plan period |
| **Purchased envelopes** | Extra packs; usable **only with an active plan**; expire **90 days** after purchase |
| **Self-sign envelopes** | **Unlimited** on every public plan; do not use your monthly envelope quota |
| **ID verification credits** | Monthly + purchased packs; consumed when a new Verify session starts |

Out of monthly envelopes: upgrade the plan or buy a pack. Send blocks with a clear error and can open a purchase overlay (**Buy envelopes or Signyd Verify**).

## Public plans

Prices are in **USD**. The headline monthly price on [signyd.com/pricing](https://signyd.com/pricing) is the **annual** rate (billed yearly). Monthly billing costs more per month.

| | Starter | Pro | Business | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | **$0**/mo (no card) | **$39**/mo annual ($468/yr) · **$49**/mo monthly | **$99**/mo annual ($1,188/yr) · **$119**/mo monthly | **$249**/mo annual ($2,988/yr) · **$299**/mo monthly |
| **Envelopes / month** | 3 | 50 | 120 | Unlimited |
| **Self-sign** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **ID verifications / month** | — | 3 | 10 | 30 |
| **Seats** | 1 | 1 | 3 | 15 |
| **Recipients / envelope** (signers + CC) | Up to 10 | Up to 40 | Up to 40 | Up to 99 |
| **Storage** | 1 GB | 20 GB | 1 TB | Unlimited |
| **Custom branding** | No | No | Yes | Yes |

All public plans include PDF sealing with **AATL**, unlimited templates, and an audit trail.

Features labeled **unlimited** (Scale envelopes, self-sign, Scale storage, templates) follow the [Fair Use Policy](/legal/fair-use). Unlimited means no fixed published monthly cap for ordinary business use — not unrestricted platform capacity or a substitute for Enterprise volume.

Need custom security, a HIPAA BAA, data residency, or sustained high volume? Use the **Enterprise** contact card on **Plans & Addons**.

## Storage and seats

Billing shows storage as **{used} of {limit}**. Your plan includes a storage quota. **Storage packs** and **extra seats** are add-ons you can add in **Plans & Addons**.

## Plans and add-ons

Open **Plans & Addons** or **Choose a plan** to see the current public plans, prices, trials, and extras. Typical extras:

- Envelope pack
- ID verification pack
- Extra seat
- Storage pack

You can switch **Monthly** or **Annual**, apply a coupon, upgrade, or downgrade from this tab.

**Starter** activates with **Activate plan**. No payment method is required.

## Billing tab

- Active plan and status
- **Cancel plan** at period end, or **Keep plan**
- **Manage billing in Stripe** (customer portal)
- **Recurring charges** and next charge date
- **Receipts** download

## Cards

Save up to **2** cards with Stripe. Mark a **Default**. Add, edit, or remove.

## Checkout

- Card payment for paid plans, USD
- Free Starter: email only, then **Activate plan**
- **Digital delivery** — plan and envelopes activate when payment confirms (or immediately for free)
- Coupon field
- Change plan / interval in the cart
- Taxes may apply

Fees are **not refundable**, except a confirmed duplicate charge. Canceling stops the next renewal; it does not prorate the current period. See [Payments and refunds](/legal/payments).
