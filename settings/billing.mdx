Routes:

- `/settings/billing` — owners and admins
- `/choose-plan` — required when there is no usable plan
- `/checkout` — pay
- `/buy/[slug]` — public plan link

Tabs: **Billing**, **Plans & Addons**, **Cards**.

## Credits

| Type | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Signing credits** | **1 credit = 1 completed signing process** |
| **Monthly credits** | Come from the active plan period |
| **Purchased credits** | Extra packs; usable **only with an active plan**; expire **90 days** after purchase |
| **ID verification credits** | Monthly + purchased packs; consumed when a new Verify session starts |

Out of credits: upgrade the plan or buy a pack. Send blocks with a clear error and can open a purchase overlay.

## Storage and seats

Billing shows storage as **{used} of {limit}**. Your plan includes a storage quota. **Storage packs** and **extra seats** are add-ons you can add in **Plans & Addons**.

## Plans and add-ons

Open **Plans & Addons** or **Choose a plan** to see the current public plans, prices (USD), trials, and extras. Typical extras:

- Signing credit pack
- ID verification pack
- Extra seat
- Storage pack

You can switch **Monthly** or **Annual**, apply a coupon, upgrade, or downgrade from this tab.

## Billing tab

- Active plan and status
- **Cancel plan** at period end, or **Keep plan**
- **Manage billing in Stripe** (customer portal)
- **Recurring charges** and next charge date
- **Receipts** download

## Cards

Save up to **2** cards with Stripe. Mark a **Default**. Add, edit, or remove.

## Checkout

- Card payment, USD
- **Digital delivery** — plan and credits activate when payment confirms
- Coupon field
- Change plan / interval in the cart
- Taxes may apply
