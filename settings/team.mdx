---
title: "Team"
description: "Invite, disable, and remove workspace members."
---

Route: `/settings/team`

Owners and admins manage the team. Members cannot open Settings.

## Invite

1. Open **Settings → Team**.
2. Enter the email.
3. Assign **Member** or **Admin**. You cannot invite a second owner.
4. Send the invite. The person joins at `/signup?invite=…` with that email locked.

Your plan includes a seat count. Extra seats are an add-on on [Billing](/settings/billing).

## Disable or remove

You can disable or remove a member. Removing a member does **not** delete documents they requested. The requester name stays on those documents as information.

You cannot change the owner from this page.
