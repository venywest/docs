---
title: "Branding"
description: "Logo, colors, and after-signing redirect when your plan includes branding."
---

Route: `/settings/branding`

Owners and admins customize how emails and the signing experience present your organization, when the plan includes branding.

Typical options:

- Workspace logo
- Accent color
- **After signing** redirect URL (also available per request on send)

Members do not see this page.
