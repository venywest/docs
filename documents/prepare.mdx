Route: `/documents/[id]/prepare`

Preparation has three steps: recipients, fields, then review and send. See [First document](/first-document) for the first-time walkthrough.

## Recipients

Add at least one signer with **email**, **first name**, and **last name**.

- **+ Add recipient**
- **Add myself**
- **Self sign** — you sign now; optionally add up to **3** people who only receive the signed file
- Pick a saved [contact](/contacts/overview)
- Toggle **Require Signyd Verify** on any signer who must verify identity before the panel

Recipient limits (signers + CC) depend on your [plan](/settings/billing):

| Plan | Max recipients / envelope |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | 10 |
| Pro | 40 |
| Business | 40 |
| Scale | 99 |

The prepare step shows **{count} / {max} recipients**. Templates can require a minimum number of signers.

### Self sign

**Self sign** is for documents you complete yourself. It does **not** use your monthly envelope quota (unlimited on every public plan, subject to [fair use](/legal/fair-use)).

1. Choose **Self sign**.
2. Optionally add up to **3** people who receive the completed file (they are not signing parties).
3. Place your fields, then finish signing.

**Add myself** on a normal multi-party send is different: you are one of the signers on an envelope that uses a monthly envelope when the process completes.

## Fields

Select a recipient, choose a field type, and click the page. Add at least one **Signature** or **Initials** field. See [Fields](/documents/fields).

## Review and send

1. Confirm **From**, **Send to**, and optional **CC** (CC receives the final document only).
2. Optional: **Request signatures in order** (sequential signing).
3. Set **Document language** (English or Español).
4. Edit **Subject** and **Message**.
5. Optional: **After signing** redirect URL if your plan includes branding.
6. Select **Send document**. Wait for **Sending for signature…**. Do not leave the page.

### After send

If you added yourself as a signer, Signyd can open your signing link immediately. Otherwise you land on [document detail](/documents/detail).

## If send fails

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No envelopes remaining / You need an envelope | Upgrade or buy an envelope pack |
| No ID verification credits remaining | Buy a verification pack or upgrade |
| Too many recipients | Remove signers or CC to stay within your plan max |
| Identity verification is not configured | Contact Signyd support |
| Sending is not configured yet | Contact Signyd support |

A **Buy envelopes or Signyd Verify** overlay can open from the send flow.
